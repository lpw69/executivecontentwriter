What an executive content writer does

An executive content writer is not a generalist who happens to have a founder on the invoice. The job is to hold a leader's voice steady across the messages that carry weight. That includes a point of view the market can test, a note the company can repeat, and a post a customer or candidate might read twice.

Some of the writing is public. LinkedIn and X are the visible part for most founders. Some of it is the judgement about what should stay private. A useful writer treats those as one problem, not two products.

Why founders choose specialists over generalists

A generalist can fill a calendar. A specialist in executive work protects meaning. The difference shows up in three places.

  • Audience. A founder writes for customers, candidates, investors and their own team, often in the same week. Those readers do not need the same sentence.
  • Consequence. A loose claim in a founder's voice is harder to walk back than a brand slogan.
  • Continuity. The voice has to sound like the same person in March and in October.

Specialists earn the hire when the cost of a wrong sentence is higher than the cost of a slow one. If you only need posts, a general writer may be enough. If your name is on the line, it usually is not.

The work itself

The paid work this site describes in public is founder-led LinkedIn and X ghostwriting, from $2,500 a month. A wider executive communications brief, such as a narrative for a leadership team, is a conversation. It is not a second price list.

A useful piece of executive content does four quiet things. It states who the company is for. It shows that the founder has done the work. It takes a position someone could disagree with. It leaves the reader knowing what to think next, even when there is nothing to buy.

Executive branding, in this sense, is not a new personality. It is the public record of judgements the company already makes. The writer’s task is to make that record legible, not louder.

How to choose

Ask who writes. Ask what the first two weeks look like. Ask how you leave if the voice is wrong. Ask what the monthly figure includes, and what would change it. If the answers are a stack of package names, you are being sold a menu.
