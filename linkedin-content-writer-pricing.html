One figure

Founder-led LinkedIn and X ghostwriting starts at $2,500 a month. That is the only price on this site. There are no tier names, and there is no menu hiding under a friendlier label.

Founders comparing ghostwriter cost usually meet three kinds of opacity. A rate that is not a rate. A package that grows after the first call. A page of numbers that still does not say who writes. This page is the opposite. You can read the figure, decide it is wrong for you, and leave. No hidden fees means we do not add a surprise price after you have agreed the work.

What that starting point is for

It is for a founder or executive who will publish in their own voice on LinkedIn and X, with us doing the writing. It is not a bundle of unrelated marketing services. It is not a retainer you have to decode.

If the brief is wider, for example a full executive communications programme for a leadership team, we scope it on a call. We do not invent a second number here to look complete. The communications guide describes that wider discipline without turning it into a price list.

How to read other options

A fair comparison of freelancers, specialists and agencies does not need a table of someone else's rates. It needs a clear account of what you are actually buying.

  • A freelancer is often engaged to produce posts, while you still supply the point of view and the management time.
  • An agency usually sells a team and a process. The person who writes can sit several steps away from you, and the figure you first heard is not always the figure you keep paying.
  • A specialist should be able to say who writes, how the voice is handled, and what the figure is, before you book.

We are the specialist case, published as one starting point: from $2,500 a month. Clients live within two weeks. There is a 21-day get-out clause if the fit is wrong. That is the whole of the commercial offer on this site. Anything else is a conversation, not a hidden line item.

What we will state

More than a billion views across socials to date. We do not name clients. We do not attach a promised pipeline to the fee. The terms you can check today are the starting figure, the two weeks, and the 21 days.
