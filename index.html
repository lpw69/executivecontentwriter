What this site is

Executive Content Writer is the executive communications practice of UDG Development Ltd. The pages here are guides. They explain how a founder or executive should think about what they say in public, and what that has to do with what they say inside the company.

The paid work we describe in public is specific. Founder-led LinkedIn and X ghostwriting, from $2,500 a month. There are no tier names on this site, and there is no second price. If a brief is wider than that, we talk about it on a call rather than inventing a menu.

Who it is for

It is for founders and executives who are the voice of a company and do not have a clear hour to draft that voice every week. It is a poor fit if you want a pile of unrelated writing services, or a promise about revenue. Clarity is the offer. A number on your pipeline is not.

How an engagement works

You book a call. We talk about the company, the readers who matter, and the limits of the voice: what you will say, and what you will not. Clients live within two weeks. If the fit is wrong, a 21-day get-out clause means neither side is stuck in a long argument.

More than a billion views across socials to date. That is the proof this site uses. We do not name clients, and we do not borrow anyone else's results.

Why the guides come first

A writing practice should be willing to show its standard before it sends an invoice. The guides cover the role of an executive writer, the audiences a leader actually has, executive communications as a discipline, thought leadership that a reader can use, and a LinkedIn framework for startup CEOs. Read the one that matches the question you already have. Then book a call if you want the work done with you.
